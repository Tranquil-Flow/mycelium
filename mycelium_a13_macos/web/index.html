Remove staged run files

04 · Stay in control
Pause, restart, or leave
Stop retains your identity, membership recovery and evidence. Restart never silently creates a replacement identity. Revoke withdraws membership authority; it is different from deleting local files.
To remove the application, quit first and move the app to Trash. Keep private state for recovery. Explicit private-state removal is documented in the runbook; no secure-erasure claim is made.
