Your key

This browser made a key. It is how swarms recognise you: no email, no password, no account. The private half never leaves this browser.

2F00-F2E5-3E8E-2E5F

Swarm owners grant access to this fingerprint. Swarms you chat with can link your prompts to it.

Forget this key? You lose every access you were given and you can no longer manage the swarms you own with it. A new key starts with a fresh allowance, but limits by network address still apply.
