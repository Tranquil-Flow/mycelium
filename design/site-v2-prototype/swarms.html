Lend another Mac

  1. Install the Mycelium app
    Apple silicon Macs only. The app is not published yet: ask a swarm owner for it.
  2. Join with the owner's invite
    The owner sends a link or QR from "Invite a Mac". They approve your Mac.
  3. See it here
    In the app press "Show on mycelium.now". This page remembers it in your browser only.

Your Mac is never listed by name for anyone else, only as a label the swarm shows. Pause or leave any time in the app.

Start another swarm

Pick a model from the registered list, choose who may use it, then attach a Mac from the app. You can own up to five swarms per browser key.

Your key

This browser made a key. It is how swarms recognise you: no email, no password, no account. The private half never leaves this browser.

2F00-F2E5-3E8E-2E5F

Swarm owners grant access to this fingerprint. Swarms you chat with can link your prompts to it.

Forget this key? You lose every access you were given and you can no longer manage the swarms you own with it. A new key starts with a fresh allowance, but limits by network address still apply.
