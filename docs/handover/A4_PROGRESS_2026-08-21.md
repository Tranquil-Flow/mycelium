## Environment gotcha that blocked this cycle (and its fix)

The Hermes terminal injects
`PYTHONPATH=~/.hermes/hermes-agent:…/venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages`. Under
that path, `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.14` imports **python3.11 wheels** and dies
on `numpy._core._multiarray_umath`, `pydantic_core._pydantic_core`, and a broken
`pytest`. Every repo python3.14 invocation — pytest, the serve launch, the node
subprocesses, the sealer — must be prefixed with `env -u PYTHONPATH`. This is
recorded in memory and is the single most likely repeat-failure trap.
