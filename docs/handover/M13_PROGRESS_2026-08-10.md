## Exact non-claims and next gate

M13 proves capability-aware allocation over the measured two-host order. It does not
claim that this order is globally optimal, does not select among three-device cycles,
and does not claim relay/direct-path intelligence. Those are M14: measured directed
cycle and transport-path selection. Different-network Mac and generic Android/Termux
participation remain conformance work and were explicitly deferred from this gate.
