# M16 completion — resource admission and bounded scheduling

## Outcome

M16 is complete for the qualified three-host, single-track physical boundary. The
live request path now performs all-stage memory/KV/workspace reservation before
dispatch, commits one immutable topology- and graph-bound path, applies a bounded
interactive/batch QoS queue with aging, and releases every reservation on completion,
cancellation, timeout, or failure.

The runtime truthfully reports `sequential_dispatch`. It does not claim continuous
batching, pipeline overlap, or physical microbatching. Those remain unavailable until
runtime traces demonstrate them.

## Frozen contracts

- `mycelium.request_gateway.v2` binds workload profile and QoS at admission.
- `mycelium.request_event.v2` emits bounded `admission`, `queue`, `prefill`,
  `first_token`, `decode`, and `completion` lifecycle events.
- `mycelium.m16_runtime_status.v1` projects privacy-reduced queue, resource,
  reservation, candidate-path, locked-path, incident, and budget state.
- `mycelium.performance_budget.v3` closes the M15 concurrency and queueing
  deferrals while retaining one explicit runtime-batch exclusion.

All request-path reservations bind request, deployment, graph digest, topology
version, placement, QoS, and expiry. Progressive candidates remain distinct from the
locked decode path in both the status contract and Network UI.

## Performance budget

The deployed `m16-performance-budget.json` records
`met_with_approved_exclusions`. Admission latency, queue wait, depth, two physical
completions, interactive regression, batch starvation, and cancellation release all
meet their frozen bounds. `runtime_batch_size` is the sole approved exclusion because
the observed runtime dispatched one request at a time. Sequential concurrency is not
relabelled as batching.

## UI and browser proof

- Inference renders server-owned admission through completion phases, workload/QoS,
  topology version, and all-stage reservation count while the request runs.
- Network renders the progressive candidate separately from the locked decode path.
- Nodes renders assigned/reserved/free memory, KV, workspace, and reservation bounds.
- Plans and Readiness render the physical budget plus its approved exclusion.
- Incidents renders overload, expiry, cancellation, timeout, and cleanup outcomes.
- Refresh retained prompt, streamed response, model/deployment attribution,
  workload/QoS, and bounded request history.

Two browser defects found during qualification were closed: native `fetch` is bound
to `globalThis`, and the M16 poller no longer aborts a slow request every 400 ms or
clears a valid status during cleanup.
