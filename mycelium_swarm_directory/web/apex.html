How a swarm works

1. Choose your Macs

An owner starts a swarm and invites people they trust. Each Mac explicitly agrees to share within its own limits.

2. Share the model

The app places model layers across the participating Macs. A one-Mac route runs the model on that Mac alone.

3. Chat at the entry

Your browser sends prompts directly to the swarm’s entry. The directory is a phone book, never the inference server.

Trust the people running your model.

The swarm can read your prompts

Participating Macs can see prompts, replies or intermediate activations. This is not end-to-end encrypted from them. Do not send secrets.

The directory never carries prompts

No account or tracking by this site. Browsing this explainer creates no browser key. The swarms app uses a key stored in your browser.

Cloudflare is in the connection

Cloudflare terminates TLS for this site and Cloudflare-hosted entries. It can see IP addresses, timing and plaintext traffic at those endpoints.

You choose when to share

Installing the app is not consent to run a model. The app asks before downloading and sharing. Resource limits and a stop control stay on your Mac.
